A free beta update for **Call of Duty: World at War (2008)**. No Patreon subscription is required.

## What's new

- **Less short-range foliage pop-in:** grass and low shrubs with unusually short cutoffs remain visible farther away. Nearby detail and normal scene visibility checks are retained.
- **Mosin clip reload refinements:** improved left-hand clip pickup and rifle stability during insertion and regripping.
- **Additional interaction work:** mechanical manual-reload sounds/controller feedback, Campaign pause-menu layout adjustments, and a safeguard against accidental melee after trigger release.

The foliage change passed both-eye Shuri Castle simulator checks, followed by a Hard Landing headset test through VirtualDesktopXR at a 90 Hz display setting. The result was substantially better, **but some pop-in remains**. This is not a claim of infinite draw distance, higher headset FPS, or complete coverage of every map.

The retained gameplay DLL passed **76 native tests**. Mosin clip work received a local headset check; the additional pause, melee and feedback changes have simulator coverage and still welcome affected-user/hardware retests. The final package has not received another full Campaign playthrough. Launcher behavior is retained from beta.4, with beta.5 version metadata.

## Download and update

Download **WorldWarVR-v0.4.0-beta.5-Portable.zip** to play. Close the game and old launcher, extract into a **new empty folder**, keep all files together, and run **WorldWarVR.exe** after connecting your headset/controllers. Do not merge builds or extract into the game installation. Select your legitimate compatible World at War 1.7 installation and start with **Performance** quality. Leave **Automatic Reload OFF** for physical reloads.

This is portable: no installer or automatic updater. The attached **Source.zip** includes complete corresponding source and pinned dependency contents, including original sources for the retained native helper. Source is optional to play and available without an extra fee. Use **SHA256SUMS.txt** or the ZIP sidecars to verify downloads. GitHub's autogenerated source archives are not the player package.

## Remaining issues and support

This update does not establish new regional-executable or all-headset compatibility. Affected startup/runtime configurations, Rift S aiming, Custom Zombies DirectX crashes, smoke flicker and remaining interaction reports still need targeted confirmation. Local/offline Multiplayer is experimental; online/public/protected servers are unsupported.

Report the exact ZIP/build, headset/connection/OpenXR runtime, quality setting, map/weapon and reproducible steps through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jplakon/WorldWarVR/issues) or your existing support conversation. Remove personal information from logs before posting. Do not upload game files, keys or saves.
