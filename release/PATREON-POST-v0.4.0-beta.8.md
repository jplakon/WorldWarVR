World War VR v0.4.0-beta.8 — Ammo Pouch & Aiming Update

Free for everyone! This update makes manual reload ammunition easier to find
and expands the model-specific aiming work across audited Campaign/Zombies guns.
Thank you to everyone supporting the project and helping test it.

What's new:

- A small near-hip AMMO/GRIP marker with gentle discovery vibration.
- A raised front-lap ammunition reach for seated play; looking down no longer tilts the pouch.
- Expanded weapon/model-specific aiming, retaining native recoil and spread.
- Optional right weapon-hand pitch/yaw/roll fit controls in the launcher.
- Refined scope camera/lens handling, including PTRS variants.

The gameplay build passed 114 native tests and focused OpenXR Tester checks,
including 203 correlated native traces across 76 ordinary weapon/model pairs.
It received positive local headset feedback. This is not all-weapon, scope-zero,
all-distance or all-headset certification; personal hand-angle settings are not
an independent sight/impact correction or an automatic Quest 2 preset.

GitHub download: https://github.com/jplakon/WorldWarVR/releases/tag/v0.4.0-beta.8

The Patreon attachment bundles the same Portable ZIP, complete corresponding
source and checksums. Use Portable.zip to play; source is optional. Close the
game/old launcher and extract into a new empty folder, not over an earlier build
or your game installation. Connect your headset/controllers and run WorldWarVR.exe.
You need your own legitimate compatible World at War 1.7 installation.

Accepted binaries are retained unchanged; internal launcher labels can still say
beta.7. Use the beta.8 download filename, checksums and component map to identify it.

Low FPS/stutter remains open, and enemy-grenade throwback stays disabled. Rare
variants, scope impacts and affected-user hardware cases still need feedback.
Regional Compatibility Test1's Campaign validation fix is not included; keep a
working Test1 folder intact. Offline Multiplayer is experimental; online play is
unsupported. Prior foliage improvements are retained.
