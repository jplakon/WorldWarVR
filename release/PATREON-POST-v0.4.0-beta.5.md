World War VR v0.4.0-beta.5 — Foliage & Reload Update

Beta.5 is ready! It's available free on GitHub, and the attachment here contains the same player download, complete matching source and checksums. Thank you to everyone supporting the work and testing the mod.

WHAT'S NEW

Grass and shrubs with unusually short draw distances now stay visible farther away. I tested the change in the OpenXR simulator and then in my headset in Hard Landing. It's much better, though some foliage pop-in still remains. I'm releasing this as an improvement, not claiming every map is completely fixed or promising a higher frame rate.

This build also refines Mosin clip pickup and rifle stability during insertion/regripping. It includes mechanical manual-reload sounds and controller feedback, Campaign pause-menu layout adjustments, and another safeguard against accidental melee after trigger release. The clip work received a local headset check; the other interaction changes have simulator coverage and still need broader hardware/reporter feedback.

The gameplay DLL passed 76 native tests, including the native distance-hook and failure-path checks. The final package hasn't received another full Campaign replay. The launcher keeps its existing behavior, with beta.5 version information.

INSTALLATION / UPDATING

1. Close World War VR and its launcher.
2. Extract the Portable ZIP into a new, empty folder—not your game folder or an older mod package.
3. Keep all files together and run WorldWarVR.exe after connecting and waking your headset/controllers.
4. Select your own legitimate compatible World at War 1.7 installation. Choose Main Menu for Campaign/Zombies and start with Performance quality.
5. Leave Automatic Reload OFF for physical reloads, or enable it if you prefer A to complete the normal reload.

There is no installer or automatic updater. Installation instructions, full controls and known issues are included.

ONGOING SUPPORT

This isn't a universal headset/runtime or regional-executable compatibility fix. Custom Zombies crashes, affected startup configurations, Rift S aiming, smoke flicker and remaining weapon/interaction reports still need targeted tests. Please report the exact build, headset/runtime, map/weapon and steps to reproduce through Discord DMs, the comments or GitHub Issues. Keep personal information out of public logs; don't upload game executables, keys or saves.
