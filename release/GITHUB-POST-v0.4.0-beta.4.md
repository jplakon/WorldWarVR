# World War VR v0.4.0-beta.4 — Chest Holstering & Reload Reliability

A new free beta for **Call of Duty: World at War (2008)**. No Patreon subscription is required.

## What's new

- **Weapons rest on your chest:** release both grips and the current weapon stays close to your chest as you turn, lean or crouch. It follows an upright headset-based torso estimate instead of remaining attached to the game's old body direction. Looking down or tilting your head does not tip the holstered weapon. Normal held aiming remains separate.
- **More reliable launcher settings:** controls wait for saved settings and game discovery to finish loading, preventing early changes from being overwritten. Failed initialization no longer saves partially loaded controls.
- **Reload-state improvements:** gameplay options are passed explicitly at launch, effective reload mode is logged, and delayed magazine-asset validation can recover without recreating engine-owned objects.

## Download and update

Download **WorldWarVR-v0.4.0-beta.4-Portable.zip** to play. Close the old game/launcher, extract into a **new empty folder**, keep all files together, and open **WorldWarVR.exe**. Do not merge builds or extract into the game installation. Select your legitimate compatible World at War 1.7 folder, connect the headset/controllers, and start with **Performance** quality. Existing normal launcher preferences/profile locations are retained; no private test-profile routing is shipped.

For manual reloads, leave **Automatic Reload OFF**. Enable it if you want A to perform the full native reload. Snap/smooth turning remains an independent option.

There is no installer or automatic updater. The attached **Source.zip** is complete matching source, including pinned dependency contents; it is optional to play. Use **SHA256SUMS.txt** or the ZIP sidecars to verify downloads. GitHub's autogenerated source archives are not the player package and omit ordinary submodule contents.

## Test scope and remaining issues

The gameplay DLL passed 72 native tests, focused simulator weapon checks, and a physical-headset chest-placement test. Reload-setting work passed five simulator and five physical Garand cases; the release launcher is version-stamped from that tested implementation. The original customer's intermittent first-launch automatic reload was not reproduced, so that report still needs their retest.

This is **not a performance update** or an all-headset compatibility fix. Index audio/no-image startup, affected Pimax/runtime configurations, Rift S sight regression, Custom Zombies DirectX crashes, regional/uncut executable compatibility, and remaining scope/pause/smoke/interaction reports are still under investigation. The Campaign was completed during earlier development; the final beta.4 package has not received another full Campaign playthrough. Local/offline Multiplayer is experimental; online/public/protected servers are unsupported.

Please report the exact build, headset/connection/OpenXR runtime, quality setting, map/weapon and steps to reproduce through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jplakon/WorldWarVR/issues) or your existing support conversation. Remove personal information before posting logs. Do not upload game files, keys or saves.
