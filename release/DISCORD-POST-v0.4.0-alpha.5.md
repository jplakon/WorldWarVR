World War VR **v0.4.0-alpha.5** is up on Patreon for the **$3/month tier and above**!

This update adds PTRS-41 physical scope/manual clip reloading, corrects
Panzerschreck rocket alignment, adds smooth right-stick tank-cannon controls
and a cannon-target crosshair, and brings in COD4-style firing rumble.

I'm still working through the Campaign, so this remains an alpha—not a claim
that every mission or headset combination is fully tested. Pimax and Meta
Quest Link compatibility issues are still under investigation.

Download the new release and full instructions here:
https://www.patreon.com/J_Play/

the Patreon comments or DM me here; logs and recordings are welcome!
