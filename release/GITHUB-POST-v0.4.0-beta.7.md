A free beta update for Call of Duty: World at War (2008), focused on jungle foliage. No Patreon subscription is required.

## What changed

- Grass and shrubs with short cutoffs remain visible farther away.
- Audited dense ground cover and selected mangrove/birch profiles retain fuller leaves and detail, addressing the reported blurry-to-full-leaf transition.
- Beta.6's controls, reload, handling and targeted sight corrections are retained.

The reported jungle foliage issue passed a local headset check after focused OpenXR Tester work. The exact retained gameplay DLL passed 102 native tests and 27,431 foliage ABI checks. Changes are targeted: ordinary visibility checks, authored tree endpoints and shared asset/texture data remain intact.

## Download and update

Download **WorldWarVR-v0.4.0-beta.7-Portable.zip** to play. Close the game and old launcher, extract into a **new empty folder**, keep all files together, then run **WorldWarVR.exe** after connecting your headset and controllers. Do not merge builds or extract into the game installation.

Select your legitimate compatible World at War 1.7 installation and start with **Performance** quality; use **Recovery** if needed. Complete corresponding source, including pinned dependencies, is in the separate **Source.zip**. Source is optional to play and free. Checksums and the GPL source notice accompany both downloads. GitHub's autogenerated source archives are not the player package.

## Still being worked on

**Low FPS and stutter remain under investigation.** Fuller foliage can be more demanding. This release excludes the later private performance experiments and does not promise COD4 VR frame rates, infinite draw distance or every-map pop-in elimination.

Practical enemy-grenade throwback remains unresolved and disabled. Existing weapon, keyboard-prompt, startup/runtime and regional compatibility cases remain open. If Regional Compatibility Test1 works for you, keep that folder intact; its launcher validation change is not included here. Local/offline Multiplayer is experimental; online/public/protected servers are unsupported.

Coverage is focused, not a new full Campaign replay or certification of every weapon/headset. Please report the exact ZIP, headset/connection/runtime, map, weapon and reproducible steps through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jplakon/WorldWarVR/issues) or your existing support conversation. Review logs for personal information; do not upload game executables, keys or saves.
