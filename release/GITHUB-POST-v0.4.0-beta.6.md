A free beta update for **Call of Duty: World at War (2008)**, focused on controller interactions and weapon aiming. No Patreon subscription is required.

## What changed

- **Fewer accidental knife attacks:** right-stick-click melee is disabled by default. Deliberate physical melee remains available; an explicit optional stick-click binding is retained.
- **More reliable sprinting:** a short left-stick click starts sprint when the native game permits it, while respecting stamina, stance and scripted restrictions.
- **Reload and handling refinements:** improved magazine/clip interactions, insertion and regripping across the tested weapon families, plus steadier weapon transitions and recovery after released-hand tracking gaps.
- **Targeted sight alignment corrections:** fixes for specific weapon profiles, including the starting Colt in Nacht der Untoten. The Colt correction was checked with actual native shots in OpenXR Tester and then confirmed in a local headset test. This is not a global aim offset applied to every weapon.
- **Controller-accessible Little Resistance radio:** selection, aiming, firing and objective progression were verified in OpenXR Tester without keyboard gameplay input.
- **Clearer VR guidance and weapon visibility:** controller-aware reload/radio hints, lower held-controls help, and a Colt shared-surface visibility correction during magazine removal.

The gameplay DLL passed **102 automated native tests** and is retained byte-for-byte from the tested build. Focused headset feedback covered sprinting, manual reload, handling, visible weapon parts, help readability and the starting Colt. Other cases have bounded Tester coverage; this is not a new complete Campaign replay or certification of every weapon, headset or controller profile. Earlier foliage, chest-holstering, scope and reload-feedback improvements are retained.

## Download and update

Download **WorldWarVR-v0.4.0-beta.6-Portable.zip** to play. Close the game and old launcher, extract into a **new empty folder**, keep all files together, and run **WorldWarVR.exe** after connecting and waking your headset/controllers. Do not merge builds or extract into the game installation.

Select your legitimate compatible World at War 1.7 installation, choose **Main Menu** for Campaign/Zombies, and start with **Performance** quality. Leave **Automatic Reload OFF** for physical reloads, or enable it if you prefer **A** to complete the native reload.

This is a portable release, with no installer or automatic updater. The attached **Source.zip** contains complete corresponding source, retained component sources and dependency contents. Source is optional to play and available without an extra fee. Use **SHA256SUMS.txt** or the ZIP sidecars to verify downloads; GitHub's autogenerated source archives are not the player download.

## Still being worked on

The experimental contextual enemy-grenade throwback shortcut remains unresolved and disabled; this release does not advertise it as fixed. Normal grenade controls are retained. Rare keyboard-only tutorial paths, additional weapon variants, affected startup/runtime configurations, regional executable compatibility, Rift S aiming reports, Custom Zombies DirectX crashes and smoke flicker still need targeted follow-up. Some foliage pop-in remains. Local/offline Multiplayer is experimental; online/public/protected servers are unsupported.

Please report the exact ZIP, headset/connection/OpenXR runtime, map, weapon and reproducible steps through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jplakon/WorldWarVR/issues) or your existing support conversation. Review logs for personal information before sharing; do not upload game executables, keys or saves.
