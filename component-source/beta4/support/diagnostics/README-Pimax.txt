WORLD WAR VR - PIMAX 32-BIT OPENXR DIAGNOSTIC BUILD

This build validates and records the actual 32-bit Pimax OpenXR manifest and
runtime DLL selected for the 32-bit World at War process.

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a new folder. Do not overwrite an older build.
2. Connect and wake the Pimax headset. Start Pimax Play/EVO and confirm the
   headset is ready.
3. Double-click START-DIAGNOSTIC.cmd.
4. In the World War VR launcher, select the correct World at War folder.
5. Leave "Quest Air Link compatibility" DISABLED. That option forces SteamVR
   and would bypass this Pimax-runtime test.
6. Select Performance quality and launch Main Menu.
7. Wait at least 20 seconds. If the menu is visible, enter gameplay briefly.
8. Close World at War and the World War VR launcher.
9. Double-click COLLECT-DIAGNOSTICS.cmd.
10. Send the newly created WorldWarVR-Pimax-Diagnostics-*.zip file.

Please include the Pimax Play/EVO version and whether Pimax was configured as
the active OpenXR runtime when you ran the test.
