WORLD WAR VR - RANDOM MELEE RETEST

This candidate contains the guarded physical-melee path already validated in
the simulator and on a Quest 3. The original reporter still needs to confirm
that ordinary weapon handling no longer produces unintended melee attacks.

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a new folder. Do not overwrite an older build.
2. Connect and wake the headset and start your normal VR runtime.
3. Double-click START-DIAGNOSTIC.cmd and enter ordinary Campaign or Zombies.
4. For at least three minutes, walk, turn, aim, fire, reload, and change grips.
   Do NOT deliberately click the right stick or perform a stabbing motion.
5. If melee activates, stop and write the exact action and approximate time in
   TEST-NOTES.txt.
6. If no false activation occurs, separately verify each intended action:
   right-stick click melee, free-hand strike, held-pistol strike, and a
   two-handed rifle/bayonet thrust when the equipped weapon supports it.
7. Close the game and launcher, then run COLLECT-DIAGNOSTICS.cmd.
8. Send the WorldWarVR-RandomMelee-Diagnostics-*.zip and tell me which intended
   melee actions worked and whether any unintended activation occurred.
