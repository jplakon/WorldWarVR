**World War VR v0.4.0-beta.2 is now available on Patreon!**

This is the SteamVR compatibility update. It fixes the world appearing upside
down or backward, inverted headset/controller movement, missing weapon motion
tracking, and the unusable pause control on the tested Quest 3 SteamVR path.

For SteamVR controllers whose left Menu button is reserved by the dashboard,
tap **B** once to pause/resume or hold **B** for one second to recenter. Use the
right stick for crouch/prone on that fallback path.

The final build passed all 69 automated checks and was physically verified in
a Quest 3 headset with correct stereo, depth, tracking, weapon motion, and
pause/resume behavior.

World War VR remains a beta. Pimax compatibility is still unconfirmed, and I
still want reports for any mission, weapon, headset, or runtime issue you find.
The download is available to Patreon members at the **$3/month tier and up**:

https://www.patreon.com/J_Play/

logs, screenshots, and recordings through Discord or to **jplakon@gmail.com**.
