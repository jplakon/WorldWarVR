WORLD WAR VR - CLIPPED PAUSE MENU RETEST

This build is for the World at War pause menu where Save and Quit or its
confirmation was off-screen and had to be selected by guessing.

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a new folder. Do not overwrite an older build.
2. Connect and wake the headset and start your normal VR runtime.
3. Double-click START-DIAGNOSTIC.cmd and enter any Campaign mission.
4. Pause with B. Check whether the complete pause menu is visible and whether
   every option can be reached by the VR pointer.
5. Select Save and Quit and check the entire confirmation screen. You may
   cancel instead of quitting after verifying it.
6. If anything is clipped, take a headset or desktop-mirror screenshot and
   describe which edge/item is missing in TEST-NOTES.txt.
7. Close the game and launcher, then run COLLECT-DIAGNOSTICS.cmd.
8. Send the WorldWarVR-PauseMenu-Diagnostics-*.zip and the screenshot.

Please also state the headset, runtime, and launcher quality preset used.
